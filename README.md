# Abhi Patel

I build software.

## Rules I work by

- If I can't explain it in plain words, I don't understand it yet.
- An honest number beats an impressive one.
- The fewer parts a thing has, the fewer ways it can break.
- Software is work you do once that keeps working while you sleep.
- Starting is cheap. Finishing is the skill.

## What I've built

**[Pragati](https://github.com/abhieq31/Pragati)** — teams lose track of who
is doing what. This puts every project, task, and person on one screen.
Open source; anyone can run their own copy for free.

**[ChurnLens](https://github.com/abhieq31/Churn)** — businesses lose customers
and find out too late. Upload your customer list; it tells you who is likely
to leave, why, and what to do. The math runs in your browser, so your data
never leaves your computer.

**[StrokeGuard](https://github.com/abhieq31/Stroke)** — answer six health
questions, see your stroke risk and the exact reasons behind it. Honest about
its limit: on this data, no model does much better, and it says so.

**[The Nail Hubs](https://github.com/abhieq31/thenailhubs)** — a real salon's
booking site, running its actual calendar today. The database makes
double-booking impossible, not just unlikely.

**[abhieq31.com](https://abhieq31.com)** — I write to find out what I
actually think.
